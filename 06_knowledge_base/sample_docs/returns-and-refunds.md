# Octank Outfitters: Returns and Refunds

Octank Outfitters is a fictional outdoor gear retailer used in this sample.

## Return window

- Most items can be returned within **45 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Footwear can be returned within **60 days**, even if it has been worn outdoors, under our Trail Tested promise.
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.
- Gift returns are refunded as store credit to the gift recipient.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your account and open **Orders**.
2. Choose **Start a return** next to the item.
3. Print the prepaid label. Returns shipped with our label cost **$6.95**, which is deducted from the refund. Returns to a store are free.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 business days** after the warehouse receives the item. Store credit is issued within 24 hours.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact support within **14 days**. We send a replacement at no cost, and you do not pay the return shipping fee.
