# Octank Outfitters: Shipping

Octank Outfitters is a fictional outdoor gear retailer used in this sample.

## Shipping options

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 4 to 7 business days | $5.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day if ordered by 1 p.m. Pacific | $29.95 |

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. Orders to Canada ship Standard only, take 7 to 12 business days, and may incur import duties that the customer pays on delivery. We do not ship to PO boxes for Overnight orders.

## Order tracking

A tracking link is emailed when the order leaves the warehouse, usually within one business day. Orders placed on weekends ship on Monday.

## Oversized items

Kayaks, canoes, and roof boxes ship by freight. Freight delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and costs a flat **$89**. The carrier calls to schedule a delivery window.
