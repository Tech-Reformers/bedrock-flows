# Octank Outfitters: Warranty and Repairs

Octank Outfitters is a fictional outdoor gear retailer used in this sample.

## Warranty coverage

- **Octank-brand tents, packs, and sleeping bags:** lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.
- **Octank-brand jackets and apparel:** 3-year warranty.
- **Other brands:** covered by the manufacturer's warranty. We help you file the claim.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, accidents, or damage from improper care, such as machine-drying a down sleeping bag on high heat.

## Repair program

Damaged Octank gear that is not covered by the warranty can be repaired for a fee. Common repairs:

| Repair | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Zipper replacement | $25 |
| Tent pole section | $15 |
| Patch on a jacket or pack | $12 per patch |

Repairs take about **3 weeks**. Ship the item to the repair center with the form from the website, or drop it off at any store.

## Proof of purchase

Warranty claims need an order number or receipt. If you bought the item in a store and lost the receipt, we can look up the purchase by the phone number or email used at checkout.
